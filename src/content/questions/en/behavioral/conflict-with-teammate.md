Interviewers want evidence that you can disagree professionally and still move the work forward.

- Pick a real conflict about the work (technical approach, priorities, ownership), not a personality clash.
- Keep the situation short and spend most of the time on your actions: a private conversation, understanding their constraints, using data or a small experiment, escalating only when needed.
- End with a measurable result and a lesson. Do not blame the teammate, and do not claim you have never had a conflict.
